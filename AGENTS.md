# Project instructions

## Scope
Build Atunbi Coterie's fashion website incrementally. Keep requirements, decisions and implementation notes in this repository. The current deliverable is the project foundation; do not interpret it as authorization to purchase services.

## Brand
Use an elegant, warm, editorial visual direction. Prioritize garments and craftsmanship. Keep language confident, personal and practical. Use mobile-first layouts and accessible navigation.

## Accuracy
Do not invent products, prices, stock, reviews, certifications, impact statistics, business policies or contact details. Mark demonstration data clearly. Distinguish current training activities from future ambitions. Never describe the business as a registered nonprofit without confirmation.

## Virtual stylist
The required experience is a separate person-like brand character who speaks with shoppers. Her name, appearance and accent are not approved yet; Tuni is a proposal. Clearly disclose AI identity. Provide shopper-activated sound, text alternatives, mute, close and keyboard controls. Never require microphone access to shop. Obtain microphone permission only when needed. Use verified catalog and policy data. Do not promise perfect fit or invent availability. Provide support escalation.

## Engineering
Choose the stack after documenting commerce and avatar needs. Keep credentials on the server or in managed secrets. Never commit secrets, customer data or payment information. Use a payment provider rather than handling card details directly. Keep dependencies and integration setup documented. Verify mobile behavior, accessibility, catalog accuracy and critical purchase flows. Report what is functional, mocked, blocked or untested.

## Collaboration
Record approved decisions in docs/decisions.md. Update requirements when scope changes. Keep changes focused and reviewable. Do not publish unsupported claims or enable paid integrations without an approved provider and budget.
