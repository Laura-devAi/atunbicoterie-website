# Atunbi Coterie Website

Project foundation for an elegant online fashion boutique with a speaking, person-like virtual stylist created specifically for the brand.

## Goal
Help customers discover distinctive ready-to-wear clothing, understand fit and craftsmanship, and purchase confidently. Share the founder's story and the brand's practical fashion-training mission accurately and respectfully.

## Success
- Customers can find a garment, understand its sizing, price and delivery terms, and complete checkout on mobile and desktop.
- Shoppers can choose to speak or type to an AI stylist and open the products she recommends.
- Product recommendations use verified catalog data and availability.
- The boutique remains usable when the assistant is closed or unavailable.
- The owner can maintain products and content without routine developer help.
- Track purchase conversion, checkout completion, email signups, assistant usefulness and repeat purchases. Set numerical targets after a baseline exists.

## Status
Planning foundation only. No storefront, payment processing, avatar service or production deployment is implemented. No technology stack or provider has been selected.

## First implementation task
Create a responsive homepage and collection/product-page prototype with explicitly labeled sample content, plus a shopper-activated virtual-stylist panel. Review the visual direction before connecting live commerce and avatar services. A text-only panel is an interim prototype, not completion of the speaking-avatar requirement.
