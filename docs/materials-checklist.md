# Materials checklist

No original images, catalog data or existing source code have been supplied to this repository. The project documents summarize the current conversation.

## Brand
- [ ] Logo files and usage rights.
- [ ] Approved palette, typography and reference websites.
- [ ] Founder biography and approved public story.
- [ ] Contact and social links.

## Each garment
- [ ] Product name, SKU, category and price/currency.
- [ ] Front, back, detail and fit photographs with usage rights.
- [ ] Fabric, care and construction details.
- [ ] Sizes, garment measurements and fit notes.
- [ ] Stock by variant and preorder status if relevant.

## Operations
- [ ] Shipping origin, destinations, processing times and rates.
- [ ] Returns/exchanges and customer support process.
- [ ] Commerce account and payment setup.
- [ ] Domain ownership and service budget.
- [ ] Privacy, voice processing and retention choices.

## Avatar
- [ ] Approved character design, name, voice and accent.
- [ ] Commercial rights for avatar and voice assets.
- [ ] Approved greeting, brand tone and support escalation.

## Training mission
- [ ] Verified current program description.
- [ ] Approved photographs and participant consent.
- [ ] Evidence for any published outcome or impact claim.
