# Implementation plan

1. Confirm launch mode: purchases, preorders or waitlist. Gather the materials checklist and approve brand and avatar direction.
2. Select commerce, hosting, catalog administration and avatar/conversation providers. Compare integration fit, accessibility, operating costs, latency and data handling. Record decisions before implementation.
3. Build a responsive visual prototype: homepage, collection, product details and an optional assistant panel. Label sample data. Review mobile and desktop layouts.
4. Integrate the catalog, sizing, bag, shipping information and sandbox checkout. Validate inventory behavior and order confirmation.
5. Integrate the speaking avatar and voice/text conversations. Connect approved catalog and policy retrieval, product cards, support fallback and microphone controls.
6. Add approved story, craftsmanship, training, contact and policy content. Configure optional email signup and agreed analytics.
7. Verify accessibility, phone layouts, provider failures, sizing uncertainty, unavailable stock, payment failure, secret handling and shopper privacy controls.
8. Review readiness and deploy to the agreed audience. Monitor errors and customer questions; use early data to establish improvement targets.

## Definition of done
A stage is complete when its stated behavior works, relevant checks pass, limitations are documented and the repository reflects the delivered scope. A mock avatar does not satisfy the live speaking-avatar milestone.
