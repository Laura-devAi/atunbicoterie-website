# Website requirements

## Business context
The founder has described a fashion business begun in Nigeria, a renewed brand direction influenced by motherhood and life changes, and plans for ready-to-wear pieces produced in Nigeria for sale in the United States. Confirm public wording before publication.

## Audience and experience
Serve women seeking expressive, elegant clothing and customers who value Nigerian craftsmanship. Audience details and price positioning require validation. Support discovery, product evaluation, purchase, support and return visits.

## Pages
Home; shop/collections; product details; founder story; craft and community; size and fit; FAQs; shipping and returns; contact; privacy information. Include bag and checkout when sales are enabled.

## Commerce requirements
- Product images, descriptions, fabrics, care, measurements, variants, prices and stock.
- Clear size selection, unavailable-state handling and bag updates.
- Processing times, delivery charges and return terms visible before payment.
- Secure provider checkout and reliable order confirmation.
- Owner-managed catalog and inventory.
- Optional email subscription with clear consent.

## Speaking virtual stylist
User-confirmed: a separate person-like character embedded in the website, speaking with shoppers to create an engaging and distinctive boutique experience.

Required capabilities:
- Shopper-activated avatar with natural speech and expressive movement.
- Voice and text input, readable responses, mute/minimize/close controls.
- Occasion, style and budget questions that lead to relevant product cards.
- Product cards linking to valid items with accurate price and availability.
- Answers grounded in catalog, size charts and approved store policies.
- Honest uncertainty and a route to human support.
- Useful loading, failure and unavailable states; shopping must remain independent of the assistant.
- AI disclosure and clear information about voice processing and retention before enabling it.

## Acceptance examples
- A birthday-dinner request returns relevant available products or explains that no suitable items were found.
- An unavailable size is never presented as available.
- An unknown shipping answer directs the shopper to approved information or support.
- Rejecting microphone access leaves text assistance and normal shopping functional.
- Closing the avatar stops speech and microphone capture.
- No speech starts automatically on page load.
- Product browsing and checkout work when the avatar provider fails.

## Quality
Responsive layouts, keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, accessible forms and text alternatives. Load avatar resources on demand so they do not block product browsing. Measure page performance and assistant latency during integration.

## Mission content
Describe verified sewing and garment-making training respectfully. Publish participant stories and images only with appropriate consent. Do not claim donations, nonprofit status or per-purchase impact without evidence.

## Measurement
Measure product engagement, add-to-bag, checkout completion, purchases, subscriptions, assistant use and satisfaction. Define privacy-respecting analytics and retention before collecting conversation data.

## Launch gates
Confirm catalog, photography rights, prices, stock, fulfillment, policies, support contact, provider budgets and ownership. Test successful and failed payment paths in a sandbox before live sales. Validate the speaking-avatar experience with real product data.
